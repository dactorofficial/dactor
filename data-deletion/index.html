Account & Personal Data Deletion Portal

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and the Nepal Individual Privacy Act 2075, Dactor provides users with complete autonomy to permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

📱 Applications Covered by this Policy

This deletion portal and statutory erasure procedure apply to all users of the following licensed applications:

Dactor – Doctor Queue & Care Package: com.dactor.patient

For patients, guardians, and general public booking doctor consultations, tracking OPD queues, and viewing lab results.

Dactor Clinic – Queue & EMR Package: com.dactor.clinic

For registered clinics, polyclinics, diagnostic labs, hospitals, and licensed doctors managing operational appointments.

⚡ Method 1: Instant In-App Self-Service Deletion

You can execute an immediate, self-service deletion of your account and personal profile directly inside either mobile app without waiting for manual human review:

  1. Open the Dactor Patient or Dactor Clinic app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile avatar or go to the Profile / Settings tab.
  3. Select Account Security & Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete Dactor Account.
  5. Confirm your identity via your registered mobile OTP or secure biometric confirmation.

Upon confirmation, all session tokens, device push IDs (FCM), and active booking tokens are immediately invalidated across all platform instances.

🌐 Method 2: Web-Based Data Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion via the web pursuant to Google Play Store requirements, submit the form below. Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) will process your request within 24–48 business hours.

⚠ Irreversible Action Confirmation

By submitting this request, you confirm that your login credentials, appointment history, stored consultation tokens, and notification subscriptions will be permanently purged.

✔ Deletion Request Logged Successfully

Your verification ticket has been generated. An automated confirmation email / SMS will be dispatched to your registered handle.

All associated personal profiles will be completely de-indexed and permanently scrubbed from production databases within 7 business days.

📄 Data Types Deleted vs. Retained (Statutory Disclosures)

Under Google Play User Data policies, developers must clearly articulate which data categories are purged immediately and what must be held under statutory law:

Data Category Action Taken Upon Deletion Statutory Retention Justification
Account & Auth Credentials Deleted Immediately Passwords, session hashes, OTP history, and biometric tokens permanently erased from Supabase Auth within 24 hours.
Device & Push Identifiers Deleted Immediately Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens and hardware UUIDs unlinked and revoked immediately.
Active Queue Tokens & Bookings Deleted Immediately Pending and upcoming consultations canceled and scrubbed from live clinic waiting queues.
Completed Clinical & Fiscal Audit Logs Anonymized / Locked Under Nepal Medical Council (NMC) regulations and Nepal tax laws, clinics must retain consultation audit trails and fiscal tax receipts for mandatory legal periods. All patient PII is anonymized into cryptographic random UUIDs with zero link back to your personal identity.
